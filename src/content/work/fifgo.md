In mid-2023 FIFGROUP decided to revamp its website and consumer app, to stay relevant, keep existing customers and reach younger ones.

## What I designed

I revamped the mobile app's full loan-contract experience: application, payment, contract renewal, late fees and contract management.

## How we got there

We looked at the product from the user and business side through stakeholder interviews, a value proposition canvas and user interviews. Three things mattered most to FIFGROUP customers: security, relevancy and accountability.

From there we improved the information structure and visual design, so users could understand what they were reading and feel secure while doing something as serious as taking on a loan.

## Result

In UAT, users completed the loan flow in under 7 minutes with zero drop-off. The revamp was released in August 2025.
