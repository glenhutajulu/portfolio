byU is Indonesia's first fully digital provider, a subsidiary of Telkomsel. I mapped the complaint journey end to end and proposed feature improvements.

## Process

I started with a UX audit, walking the weak-signal complaint journey myself. I then ran usability tests and interviews with 5 people who matched the byU user criteria.

The journey split into three stages: awareness, exploration and complaint. The first two were relatively smooth, so I focused on the complaint stage.

## What people told us

- It takes too many questions to reach an agent.
- Some participants needed guidance to find the speed test.
- Some wanted a quicker, simpler solution than the one offered.
- Some felt the data collected earlier was wasted once an agent took over.

## How might we provide a quicker solution?

**Show the default solution first.** This step is already in the agent SOP and in the help articles. Product team data showed 95% of weak-signal complaints were solved by these steps, so the bot could offer it up front. High priority, low effort.

**Reassess the 10 questions.** Some answers can come from back-end data and others are unnecessary. The set could drop to 7.

**Check the signal directly.** A comparison with the MyXL app showed a quicker model: it refreshes the connection based on the number, confirms when the process is complete, and only asks for a form if the problem remains.

## How might we guide people to the speed test?

The speed test already existed in the app, but users did not know about it or where to find it. I added an entry point inside the chat that takes people straight to the feature.
