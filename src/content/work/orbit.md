Telkomsel Orbit is a fixed wireless internet service. I supported a major website revamp across structure, content and system integration.

## What I did

**Website structure.** I ran heuristic evaluations and user testing to simplify navigation and improve content hierarchy.

**Content.** I reworked copy and visual structure for readability and product clarity across key pages, including Home, Product and Support.

**User flows.** With the tech team, I redesigned the login, customer purchase and modem pairing journeys on the website, to simplify onboarding for new users.

**MyTelkomsel integration.** With the product owners, I worked on cross-platform UX between Orbit and the MyTelkomsel app, particularly account syncing and modem status visibility.
